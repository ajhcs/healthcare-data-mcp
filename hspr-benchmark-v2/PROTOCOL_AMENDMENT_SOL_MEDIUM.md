# Protocol amendment: Sol Medium supplement

Amendment time: 2026-07-27 UTC, after eight Luna trials had completed and their
descriptive results had been inspected.

At the user's request, the benchmark adds two subscription-native exploratory
arms using `gpt-5.6-sol` at medium reasoning: HSPR available and HSPR absent.
They answer the exact two protected questions already completed by all four Luna
arms. Every other answer-runtime, live-source, packet, isolation, trace, and
scoring condition remains matched. The exact question subset and its digest are
kept in the protected active root rather than published in Git.

This is a post-hoc exploratory supplement, not part of the original frozen
confirmatory design. Its outcomes may be displayed beside the Luna results but
must be labeled separately and cannot be used to rewrite the preregistered
primary comparisons or power analysis. The two Sol arms use one fresh trial per
question, for four trials total. No Sol key builder or scorer may run these
answer arms.

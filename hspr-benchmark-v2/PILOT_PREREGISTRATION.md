# Disjoint pilot preregistration

Pilot systems: Mass General Brigham, Mayo Clinic, CommonSpirit Health, and HCA
Healthcare. They are excluded from the confirmatory 100-system cohort.

The pilot has 12 questions (three per system) and four arms. It calibrates
missing-metric handling, score variance, tool/latency variance, and exact power.
The confirmatory sample, questions, tolerances, arm config, repetition count,
and analysis code will be frozen after adjudication and before any confirmatory
answer run. Pilot answers may not be reused in the confirmatory analysis.

Release blockers: cleanup CI green; two independent keys complete; high-reasoning
adjudication complete; HSPR identity packet complete; structural and key-aware
leakage audits pass; answer contexts demonstrably exclude `.benchmark-sealed`.
